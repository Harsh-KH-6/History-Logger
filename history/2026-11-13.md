# Today in History - November 13

## Event 1
📅 Year: 1940  
📝 Event: Walt Disney's animated musical film Fantasia is first released at New York's Broadway Theatre, on the first night of a roadshow.

## Event 2
📅 Year: 1864  
📝 Event: American Civil War: The three-day Battle of Bull's Gap ends in a Union rout as Confederates under Major General John C. Breckinridge pursue them to Strawberry Plains, Tennessee.

## Event 3
📅 Year: 1918  
📝 Event: World War I: Allied troops occupy Constantinople, the capital of the Ottoman Empire.
