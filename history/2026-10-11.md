# Today in History - October 11

## Event 1
📅 Year: 1910  
📝 Event: Piloted by Arch Hoxsey, Theodore Roosevelt becomes the first U.S. president to fly in an airplane.

## Event 2
📅 Year: 1776  
📝 Event: American Revolution: A fleet of American boats on Lake Champlain is defeated by the Royal Navy, but delays the British advance until 1777.

## Event 3
📅 Year: 1958  
📝 Event: NASA launches Pioneer 1, its first space probe, although it fails to achieve a stable orbit.
