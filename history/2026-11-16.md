# Today in History - November 16

## Event 1
📅 Year: 1822  
📝 Event: American Old West: Missouri trader William Becknell arrives in Santa Fe, New Mexico, over a route that became known as the Santa Fe Trail.

## Event 2
📅 Year: 1272  
📝 Event: While travelling during the Ninth Crusade, Prince Edward becomes King of England upon Henry III of England's death, but he will not return to England for nearly two years to assume the throne.

## Event 3
📅 Year: 1797  
📝 Event: The Prussian heir apparent, Frederick William, becomes King of Prussia as Frederick William III.
