# Today in History - October 26

## Event 1
📅 Year: 2004  
📝 Event: Rockstar Games releases Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas for the PlayStation 2 in North America, which sold 12 million units for the PS2, becoming the console's best-selling video game.

## Event 2
📅 Year: 1991  
📝 Event: Three months after the end of the Ten-Day War, the last soldier of the Yugoslav People's Army leaves the territory of the Republic of Slovenia.

## Event 3
📅 Year: 1999  
📝 Event: The United Kingdom's House of Lords votes to end the right of most hereditary peers to vote in Britain's upper chamber of Parliament.
