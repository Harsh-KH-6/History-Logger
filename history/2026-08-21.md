# Today in History - August 21

## Event 1
📅 Year: 1971  
📝 Event: A bomb exploded in the Liberal Party campaign rally in Plaza Miranda, Manila, Philippines with several anti-Marcos political candidates injured.

## Event 2
📅 Year: 1879  
📝 Event: The locals of Knock, County Mayo, Ireland report their having seen an apparition of the Virgin Mary. The apparition is later named "Our Lady of Knock" and the spot transformed into a Catholic pilgrimage site.

## Event 3
📅 Year: 1944
