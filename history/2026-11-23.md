# Today in History - November 23

## Event 1
📅 Year: 1914

📝 Event: MS Explorer, a cruise liner carrying 154 people, sinks in the Antarctic Ocean south of Argentina after hitting an iceberg near the South Shetland Islands. There are no fatalities.
