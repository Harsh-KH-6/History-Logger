# Today in History - September 06

## Event 1
📅 Year: 1943  
📝 Event: Pennsylvania Railroad's premier train derails at Frankford Junction in Philadelphia, killing 79 people and injuring 117 others.

## Event 2
📅 Year: 394  
📝 Event: Battle of the Frigidus: Roman emperor Theodosius I defeats and kills Eugenius the usurper. His Frankish magister militum Arbogast escapes but commits suicide two days later.

## Event 3
📅 Year: 1940  
📝 Event: King Carol II of Romania abdicates and is succeeded by his son Michael. General Ion Antonescu becomes the Conducător of Romania.
