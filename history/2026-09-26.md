# Today in History - September 26

## Event 1
📅 Year: 1687  
📝 Event: Morean War: The Parthenon in Athens, used as a gunpowder depot by the Ottoman garrison, is partially destroyed after being bombarded during the Siege of the Acropolis by Venetian forces.

## Event 2
📅 Year: 1688  
📝 Event: The city council of Amsterdam votes to support William of Orange's invasion of England, which became the Glorious Revolution.

## Event 3
📅 Year: 1959  
📝 Event: Typhoon Vera, the strongest typhoon to hit Japan in recorded history, makes landfall, killing 4,580 people and leaving nearly 1.6 million others homeless.
