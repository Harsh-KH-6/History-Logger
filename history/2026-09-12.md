# Today in History - September 12

## Event 1
📅 Year: 1906  
📝 Event: The Newport Transporter Bridge is opened in Newport, South Wales by Viscount Tredegar.

## Event 2
📅 Year: 1959  
📝 Event: Bonanza, the first regularly scheduled TV program presented in color, is launched in the United States.

## Event 3
📅 Year: 1942  
📝 Event: World War II: First day of the Battle of Edson's Ridge during the Guadalcanal Campaign. U.S. Marines protecting Henderson Field are attacked by Imperial Japanese Army troops.
