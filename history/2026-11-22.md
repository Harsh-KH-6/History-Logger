# Today in History - November 22

## Event 1
📅 Year: 1989  
📝 Event: NASA launches Space Shuttle Discovery on STS-33, a classified mission for the United States Department of Defense.

## Event 2
📅 Year: 1942  
📝 Event: World War II: Battle of Stalingrad: General Friedrich Paulus sends Adolf Hitler a telegram saying that the German 6th Army is surrounded.

## Event 3
📅 Year: 1967  
📝 Event: UN Security Council Resolution 242 is adopted, establishing a set of the principles aimed at guiding negotiations for an Arab-Israeli peace settlement.
