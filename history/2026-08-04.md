# Today in History - August 04

## Event 1
📅 Year: 1987  
📝 Event: The Federal Communications Commission rescinds the Fairness Doctrine which had required radio and television stations to give equal time to opposing views.

## Event 2
📅 Year: 1965  
📝 Event: The Constitution of the Cook Islands comes into force, giving the Cook Islands self-governing status within New Zealand.

## Event 3
📅 Year: 1701  
📝 Event: Great Peace of Montreal between New France and First Nations is signed.
