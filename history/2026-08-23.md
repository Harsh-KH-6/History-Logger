# Today in History - August 23

## Event 1
📅 Year: 1939  
📝 Event: World War II: Nazi Germany and the Soviet Union sign the Molotov-Ribbentrop Pact. In a secret protocol to the pact, Poland, Finland, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, and Romania are divided into German and Soviet "spheres of influence".

## Event 2
📅 Year: 1382  
📝 Event: The Golden Horde, led by Khan Tokhtamysh, begins the Siege of Moscow, which ends four days later with the storming of the city and the death of Muscovite Prince Ostei.

## Event 3
📅 Year: 79  
📝 Event: Mount Vesuvius begins stirring, on the feast day of Vulcan, the Roman god of fire.
