# Today in History - November 15

## Event 1
📅 Year: 2007  
📝 Event: Cyclone Sidr hits Bangladesh, killing an estimated 5,000 people and destroying parts of the world's largest mangrove forest, the Sundarbans.

## Event 2
📅 Year: 1884  
📝 Event: The Berlin Conference of 1884-1885 met on 15 November 1884, and after an adjournment concluded on 26 February 1885, with the signature of a General Act, regulating the European colonisation and trade in Africa during the New Imperialism period.

## Event 3
📅 Year: 1864  
📝 Event: American Civil War: Union General William Tecumseh Sherman begins his March to the Sea through Georgia towards the city of Savannah.
