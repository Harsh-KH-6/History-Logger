# Today in History - August 27

## Event 1
📅 Year: 1916  
📝 Event: World War I: The Kingdom of Romania declares war on Austria-Hungary, entering the war as one of the Allied nations.

## Event 2
📅 Year: 1813  
📝 Event: French Emperor Napoleon I defeats a larger force of Austrians, Russians, and Prussians at the Battle of Dresden.

## Event 3
📅 Year: 2003  
📝 Event: The first six-party talks, involving South and North Korea, the United States, China, Japan and Russia, convene to find a peaceful resolution to the security concerns of the North Korean nuclear weapons program.
